A few ground rules.

Last updated 7 November 2026.

The music

BAJAA chooses music from a curated catalogue based on your room preferences. Music plays through YouTube’s embedded player. BAJAA does not host or sell the recordings, remove advertisements, or guarantee that every video is available in your location.

Use of the player is subject to YouTube’s Terms of Service. A BAJAA session does not grant a public-performance or commercial-use licence. Obtain any permissions required for your event or venue.

Accounts and history

Google sign-in is required. Use your own Google account. History is automatically saved as you listen; you can replay a session or delete it. Keep access to your device and account secure.

Availability

Playback requires an internet connection and a supported browser. It may pause when you leave the tab. Playback, saved history and sign-in can be interrupted by network problems or third-party services. This release does not offer offline playback, a paid subscription, or a service-level guarantee.

Respect the service

Do not attempt to access another person’s history, bypass authentication, scrape restricted content, or disrupt the service. Song titles, artist names and recordings belong to their respective owners.
