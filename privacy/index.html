Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated 7 November 2026.

Sign-in is required

You must sign in with Google to listen. The public website explains the service; the server returns playback data only after it verifies your account. History from an earlier guest version may still exist on this browser and is copied to your account on sign-in.

Google sign-in and history

Neon Auth handles Google sign-in. BAJAA receives your account identifier, name and email. We use your account identifier to save room preferences and listening history in Neon Postgres. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.

Legacy guest sessions on this device are copied to your account when you sign in. Account history is available only after an authenticated request for that account. You can delete individual sessions from History. Signing out ends the browser session; previously issued short-lived access tokens expire separately.

YouTube playback

BAJAA embeds YouTube videos for playback. Loading and using a player sends information such as your IP address and browser details to Google/YouTube. YouTube may set cookies and apply its own account, advertising and privacy settings. See Google’s privacy policy.

Local feedback

The optional music-tuning page saves cut suggestions on this device. It does not currently send suggestions to a server. BAJAA does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics in this build. Hosting and backend providers may retain operational request logs.

Your data

Delete a session from History to remove it from your account and its local account cache. Account caches are separated by account ID and remain on the device after sign-out; clear browser site data on a shared device. You can clear guest history through your browser’s site-data settings. Export your history or delete all listening history from the History page. To delete your Google-linked BAJAA account, contact contact@interncell.com. Pending sync retries stay on this device until successful or until you delete history.
