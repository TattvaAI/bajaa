# BAJAA design direction

BAJAA means “play it.” Its identity comes from printed Indian gig posters: warm paper, ink, vermilion, a wide heavy wordmark, vinyl grooves, sharp edges and restrained hard shadows. No decorative telemetry, fake testimonials, gradients, animated waveforms or scroll effects.

The product keeps NAUX's four room questions: languages, optional sound, mood, crowd. Google sign-in is compulsory; the server verifies access before returning the music library. The four questions lead to automatic playback when a gesture is available, or one play button for a deep link. There is no tracklist editor, song picker, reorder, or playlist naming flow. History saves itself. A mood change adjusts the future music; the current song stays.

Tokens: paper #f2f0e8, ink #20201e, accent #ba321f, secondary #64615a, dividers #c9c6bc. Self-hosted Archivo Black for identity, DM Sans for readable controls. Mobile gutters 20px, desktop 48px. No video panel in the final requested UI. Two YouTube decks are hidden and crossfade over 1.5 seconds, with direct cuts on iOS. Static song titles, a quiet disc graphic and one clock update per second.

Applied skills: industrial-brutalist-ui and redesign-existing-projects for this rebrand; earlier verified shortlist was Anthropic frontend-design, Vercel web-design-guidelines, UI UX Pro Max and local design-taste-frontend. Skills are guidance, not proof of quality. Current art is a CSS/vector-native record graphic. All repeated stock photographs and their files were removed; the catalogue uses text rows with thin rules and a vermilion arrow. No generation API calls.

The marketing page was removed at the user’s request for a minimal app. BAJAA domain/trademark clearance remains unverified. The name and visual identity differ from NAUX; inherited music metadata and code still require an ownership/provenance review before a commercial launch.
